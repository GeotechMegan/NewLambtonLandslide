Goal 1: Government agencies wholly rehabilitate the entire Site, and surrounds if necessary, and make good for continued residential use without the need for legal action.
Task I.D. Task to achieve Goal 1 Assigned to Status
G1T1
Discover existing mechanisms whereby government bodies are required to make good the Site.
There are currently no lawful mechanisms whereby any part of the Local or State government are required to rehabilitate private land, or an area of land comprising part privately-owned land, part public land.
G1T2
Understand Council's stance on rehabilitating the entire Site.
Newcastle Council have no intention of undertaking any works on private land, or of designing any works that will or may even have a side effect of improving private land. Source: "the recovery, reinstatement or repair of private property is the responsibility of landowners and their insurers." from NCC Landslip incident FAQs; comments made during meetings with residents; the fact that the geotechnical scope from Robert Carr & Associates (RCA ref 17653-101/1, available on the Files & Sources pg) limits the geotechnical investigation to public assets and "stabilisation", not rehabilitation: "RCA understand that CoN require a geotechnical investigation of the landslide to provide...concept options for stabilisation with specific focus on the public assets (Russell Road, Victoria Street and Baker Street).", 9/10 installed borehole locations were on public land (can anyone confirm that for me? -MM)
