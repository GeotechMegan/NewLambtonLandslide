Goal 1: Government agencies wholly rehabilitate the entire Site, and surrounds if necessary, and make good for continued residential use without the need for legal action.
Task I.D. Task to achieve Goal 1 Assigned to Status
G1T1
Identify existing statutory, policy or funding mechanisms whereby government bodies are enabled to make good the Site.
There are currently no lawful mechanisms whereby any part of the Local or State government are required to rehabilitate private land, or an area of land comprising part privately-owned land, part public land. Beyond being required, all potential candidates have signalled unwillingness/refusal to engage in site-wide remediation.
G1T2
Confirm City of Newcastle’s stated position on site-wide restoration to residential use.
Newcastle Council have no intention of undertaking any works on private land, or of designing any works that will or may even have a side effect of improving private land. Source: "the recovery, reinstatement or repair of private property is the responsibility of landowners and their insurers." from NCC Landslip incident FAQs; comments made during meetings with residents; the fact that the geotechnical scope from Robert Carr & Associates (RCA ref 17653-101/1, available on the Files & Sources pg) limits the geotechnical investigation to public assets and "stabilisation", not rehabilitation: "RCA understand that CoN require a geotechnical investigation of the landslide to provide...concept options for stabilisation with specific focus on the public assets (Russell Road, Victoria Street and Baker Street).", 9/10 installed borehole locations were on public land (can anyone confirm that for me? -MM)

General Comments
Status Definitions
Not yet examined – No material has been reviewed
Under review – Material identified but not yet assessed
Evidence identified – Relevant documents/data exist
Evidence documented – Evidence archived on site or linked
Position stated (verbal) – Position expressed verbally only
Position stated (written) – Position stated in writing
Position unclear / contested – Conflicting or ambiguous positions
Confirmed – Supported by documentary evidence
Disputed – Evidence exists on both sides
Not supported by evidence – Claim examined and not substantiated
Requires expert validation – Needs technical or legal confirmation
